Privacy Policy

Starter policy page for the website build.

Recommendations and country

BuySell.Best may use a randomly generated anonymous visitor ID, listing interactions such as views, searches, reactions and “Show me more like this”, and country-level information supplied by the hosting network to personalize recommendations and currency display. We do not need your precise GPS location for this feature.

You can reset the anonymous recommendation history from the homepage. Resetting creates a new visitor ID and asks the service to delete the stored recommendation activity associated with the previous ID when the database is connected.

Information

The production platform may collect information needed to create, secure, moderate and display listings, plus technical information required to keep the service reliable.

Currency display

Prices may show the original listing currency together with an approximate converted amount for the visitor’s detected country. Exchange rates are indicative and may differ from the amount a seller actually requests.

Advertising

When advertising partners are enabled, they may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

Contact

Use the contact page for privacy questions. This starter page should be reviewed and finalized for the final business and jurisdiction before launch.
